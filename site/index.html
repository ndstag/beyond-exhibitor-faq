Portal & Support

What is the Exhibiting Partner Portal?

The portal is where you manage everything for the event: event details, required forms, deadlines, booth staff registration, and ordering. Your login details are emailed to the Exhibitor Logistics contact we have on file.

How will you communicate with us?

We send all details, marketing opportunities and deadlines through the Exhibiting Partner Portal. Please mark these emails as safe and review all attachments.

Let us know if your contact details change. Upgrade Events, Inc. is not responsible for missed deadlines or opportunities.

Where can I get help on-site?

From Monday, May 31 to Friday, June 4, 2027, visit the Exhibitor Registration Desk on the 5th floor, at the back left-hand side of the ballroom. The Ortiz & Co. General Service Contractor Desk is right next to it.

Who is the General Service Contractor?

Ortiz & Co. handles booth and logistics services, including advance warehousing, furniture rental, and labor for booth setup and teardown.

Their Exhibitor Kit login is emailed to your Primary Booth Logistics Contact, who will need to set up an Ortiz & Co. account to place orders. You can also open the Exhibitor Kit from the Exhibiting Partner Portal. Check your spam or junk folder if you don't see it.

Shipping

Where do I ship my booth materials?

Ship booth items and all supporting materials to the Advance Warehouse, which delivers them to your booth at the Fairmont Austin.

Shipments must arrive between April 26 and May 14, 2027. Nothing is accepted before April 26, and a 30% surcharge applies to shipments received after May 14. Shipping labels, required forms and material handling charges are in the Ortiz & Co. Exhibitor Kit.

Can a truck deliver directly to the hotel?

Yes, by appointment only, starting Tuesday, June 1, 2027 at 8:00 a.m. The hotel will refuse freight that arrives earlier, and any charges for accepted early freight are yours. Certified weight tickets must accompany every shipment.

Direct-to-show shipments carry an additional 20% on top of the published material handling rates.

Can I ship small boxes straight to the hotel?

Boxes under 30 lbs can go to the FedEx Office Print & Ship Center at the Fairmont, phone (512) 474-2181. You are responsible for all shipping and delivery costs, and we can't help with pick-up or tracking. Contact FedEx directly with shipping questions.

Hours (subject to change): Monday – Friday 8:00 a.m. – 5:30 p.m., Saturday 8:00 a.m. – 4:00 p.m., closed Sunday.

Can I bring materials in my own vehicle?

Yes, starting Tuesday, June 1, 2027. Load and unload at the hotel's front entrance or the side entrance next to the loading dock bays at 100 Sabine Street.

  • No hotel or event staff will be available to help.
  • Hotel luggage carts are not allowed.
  • Only two-wheeled dollies are permitted, and you must bring your own.
  • Follow all posted parking signs and time limits. The conference is not responsible for towed vehicles.

Food & Drink Sampling

Do I need approval to hand out food or beverages?

Yes. Please visit your portal to tell us in advance if you plan to distribute any food, beverages, supplements, or elixirs. All items must be approved and meet city and county guidelines.

Do I need a permit to sample food, drinks, supplements, or elixirs?

Yes, if your samples are not in sealed, prepackaged individual servings. Austin Public Health requires a Temporary Food Permit, and we obtain it on your behalf.

Cost$64.00, invoiced through Stripe (subject to change by the City of Austin). Log into your portal to get access to the payment link.
DeadlineApril 30, 2027
Sample sizeNo more than 6 ounces
  • No permit needed: single servings handed out unopened, still sealed in their original packaging.
  • Permit needed: anything you open, pour or portion at your booth, and anything that isn't prepackaged.
  • Permits can be picked up at the Exhibitor Registration Desk.
Do I need a handwashing station?

If you require a permit to sample, you must also have a Handwashing Station. Set it up inside your booth and keep it there for the entire event. It must include:

  • Potable water, raised off the ground
  • A wastewater container (five-gallon capacity)
  • Liquid hand soap
  • Paper towels (rolls or single-use)
  • A garbage can

We don't provide handwashing stations, and they can't be rented, so plan to bring your own. An example can be found in your portal in the Exhibitor Guide.

Policies

What is the cancellation policy?

No refunds. Your signed Exhibiting Partnership Agreement is a binding contract. Payments are non-refundable, non-transferable, and can't be applied as credit toward another event.

Can I share or sublet my booth?

Subletting is not permitted. Sharing is allowed only with divisions of your own organization, with approval in advance.

What are the booth conduct rules?
  • Don't attach anything to walls, doors, floors or columns. You are responsible for any damage.
  • Stay within your booth footprint and keep aisles clear.
  • Keep brochures and flyers inside your own booth.
  • Follow local labor rules for installing and dismantling your booth.

The conference may modify or remove any exhibit, and violations can void your right to the space without refund.

Will I be photographed or recorded?

You may be. Photos, audio and video from the conference may be used to promote future events. Attending implies your consent.
